This file makes Marquee Player portable.

While portable.txt is beside MarqueePlayer.exe, Marquee Player keeps everything it saves
(settings, your library with its plays, favourites and playlists, the caches, the log) in the
Data folder beside it, and writes nothing to your user profile or to the registry. Copy
the whole folder, Data included, to take your library with you, for example on a USB
stick. Music folders on the same drive are found again when the drive has another letter.

Delete this file to have Marquee Player keep its files in your user profile instead
(%APPDATA%\MarqueePlayer), like the installed edition. The Data folder is then left
alone.
